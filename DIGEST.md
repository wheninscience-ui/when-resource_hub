THIS MONTH'S SELECTION

- Funding and fellowships: Grand Union DTP studentships for Black British students. ESRC doctoral studentships for Black British students in the social sciences at Oxford, the Open University and Brunel. Full fees and maintenance. https://granduniondtp.site.ox.ac.uk/studentships-black-british-students
- Help with costs: LMS Caring Supplementary Grants. Contribution to caring costs so that parents and carers can attend conferences, research schools or research visits in mathematics. Up to £200. https://www.lms.ac.uk/grants/caring-supplementary-grants
- Part-time and family: Shared Parental Leave and Pay. Eligible parents can share leave and statutory pay in the first year after a birth, adoption or surrogacy. Up to 50 weeks of leave. https://www.gov.uk/shared-parental-leave-and-pay
- Inclusion: Global Talent visa. UK visa route for leaders and potential leaders in academia and research, including holders of many major fellowships. https://www.gov.uk/global-talent
- Housing and living: Renters' Rights Act: overview for tenants. Official guidance on the private renting changes in England from 1 May 2026, including rolling tenancies, rent increases and the end of section 21. https://www.gov.uk/guidance/renters-rights-act-overview-for-tenants
